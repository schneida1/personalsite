Personal Advocacy
"Getting diagnosed with T1D changed what I thought products were actually for."

I have Type 1 Diabetes. That means I'm looking at glucose readings all day, adjusting on the fly, and trying to make sense of data that never really stops coming. Most of the tools built for this are clinical interfaces designed for doctors, not for people trying to live their lives.

That's what the Time In Range Project is about — and why I'm building Betawise. Not an abstract mission statement, just a real problem I deal with every day, and a skill set that's useful for solving it.
