Personal Advocacy
"Living with T1D turned my product instincts toward something that really matters."

I was diagnosed with Type 1 Diabetes, and that experience fundamentally shaped how I think about data, products, and impact. Managing T1D means living inside a constant stream of glucose readings — and too often, that data is overwhelming without the right tools to interpret it.

The Time In Range Project is my attempt to close that gap — applying the same product and AI thinking I use professionally to help people with T1D spend more time in a healthy glucose range and less time managing crisis moments.
