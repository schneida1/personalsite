Betawise

Your data, finally making sense.

I have Type 1 diabetes. My CGM takes a reading every five minutes, and I see my endocrinologist for fifteen minutes a few times a year. Betawise is the coach in between, built for the person living with it, not just the clinic.

    Jarvis

    The best input is the one you already use.

    Every notes app asks you to open it, pick a folder, and fill in a form. The fastest thing anyone does on a phone is send a text. So Jarvis is a notes app you use by texting yourself.

    9:41
    J
    Jarvis
    N-0025just now

      Try it. Type in the phone.

      No fields, no folders. Jarvis reads the message and files it.

        A simplified JavaScript port of Jarvis's Swift parser. The app uses Apple's date detection, which understands far more phrasings.

        Syntax instead of forms.

        #tags, "remind me…", "- sub-items", and #event come straight from plain text. The message itself sets the structure.

        Trade-off: syntax is hard to discover, so it's forgiving ("at 9am", "tomorrow 3pm", "in 30 minutes") and the app teaches by example.

        Capture from anywhere.

        Siri, the Action button, Shortcuts, an iMessage to yourself, or Jarvis's own phone number (via Twilio and a Cloudflare Worker).

        Four views, one source of truth.

        Feed, Board, To-do, and Calendar read from one store, so they always agree.

        Offline first.

        A full copy lives on the phone; sync with the web happens quietly in the background.

        Glanceable.

        Lock Screen widgets and a Live Activity mean you rarely need to open the app.

        How I work

        Simple is hard. That's the job.

        01

        02

        04

        A working thing settles in ten minutes what a document argues about for ten days. I build what I propose.
