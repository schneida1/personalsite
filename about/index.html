About

I'm a product manager in the New York area. I like making complicated things feel simple, and lately I've been spending my evenings learning how far AI can take a PM who wants to build.

How I try to work

A few things I keep coming back to.

/01

Show less, so people do more.

One clear next step usually beats a dashboard of twelve numbers.

/02

Meet people where they already are.

Fit into habits people already have before asking them to learn new ones.

/03

Let AI read; keep people deciding.

Models are great at summarizing. Where it matters, the final call should stay human.

/04

Prototype early.

Something people can click settles questions faster than a document can.
